Paired Core-side requirement (see core/src/main.cpp): Windows builds must call
timeBeginPeriod(1) for the I2C burst mailbox to run at its designed speed.
Measured live 2026-08-26: without it, every sleep_for()/wait_for() with a

bursts/s and a full SSD1306 frame (display.display()) dropped from
~0.84-0.91s wall to ~0.12-0.19s wall (measured via the
.codex-validation/i2c-throughput probe firmware).
